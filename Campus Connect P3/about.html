Who We Are

Campus Connect is a Johannesburg-based student organization focused on connecting students from universities and colleges across the city.

Instead of focusing on one institution, Campus Connect aims to create connections between students from different campuses and provide access to opportunities, activities and useful resources.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help students connect with people, opportunities, events and resources that can contribute to their campus experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a connected student community across Johannesburg where students can discover opportunities beyond their individual campuses.

Who We Serve

  • University students
  • College students
  • Student clubs and societies
  • Student organizations
  • Potential community and event partners
