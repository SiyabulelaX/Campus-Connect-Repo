Organisation Name: Campus Connect 
Organisation Overview 
Campus Connect is a fictional Johannesburg-Based student organization focused on connecting students from universities and colleges across the city. Instead of focusing on one institution, Campus Connect aims to create connections between students from different campuses and provide access to opportunities, activities and useful resources 

Mission and Vision Statements 
Our mission is to help students connect with people opportunities, events and resources that can contribute to their campus experience

Our Vision is creating a connected student community across Johannesburg where students can discover opportunities beyond their individual campuses 

Target Audience 
•	University Students 
•	Student Organizations 
•	Student Clubs, Societies 
•	Potential Community and Event Partners  

Web Site Goals and Objectives
•	 Simplify campus life by bringing academic, social and transport information into one platform. 
•	Promote student clubs, events and workshops Facilitated by Campus Connect 
•	Improve Campus engagement across Johannesburg educational institutions   
•	Provide useful resources in one accessible location
•	Create opportunities for collaboration between students, organizations and potential partners 
•	Encourage Student participation in communities and activities

Design And User Experience 

The Campus Connect website is designed to have a modern, welcoming and student-focused aesthetic. The overall design will help foster a sense of connection, community, and professionalism. The colour, typography, layout and navigation are consistent throughout the site to establish a recognisable Campus Connect brand.

 Colour Scheme

The proposed colour palette is based on the Campus Connect branding and is used consistently throughout the website. Navigation bar, headings, buttons, backgrounds and other significant elements of the interface are generally in the main colours.

The color palette helps create a cohesive visual identity throughout the various pages, as well as make important information and interactive elements more prominent. The colours are also chosen to give an energetic and friendly look that would suit a student-oriented organisation.

Typography

The website employs clear and easy to read fonts so that pupils can access the information easily. A hierarchy is created by using various font sizes and weights.

Larger, more prominent text are used for the main headings, which introduce each section.
Use subheadings to differentiate between different categories of information.
Body text uses simple, readable text that is smaller in size for descriptions or general information.
All buttons and navigational elements have clear and obvious text to identify interactive elements.

This typography hierarchy enables users to easily scan the page and find the information that is most relevant.

 Layout and Design

The website follows a logical structure with clearly defined sections. Images, text, cards and buttons are placed in locations that avoid the pages being overcrowded.

All navigation pages are consistent throughout the website so users can easily navigate from page to page including the Home, About Us, Services, Student Communities, Events, Transport, Announcements and Contact pages.

CSS is used to control the positioning, spacing, sizing, backgrounds, borders, buttons and other visual elements. The consistent design helps to form a unified website, as each page does not look like a different one.

User Experience Considerations

The design will be geared towards simplicity and ease of use for students. Information is organised by function to help users easily locate information like transport information, events, communities and announcements.

Headings are clear, text is readable, navigation is consistent, and buttons are noticeable. Spacing and organized sections will also help minimize visual clutter, making the information easier to scan.

The design also considers different devices and employs CSS layout methods that can be scaled across different screen sizes. This means that students can access the website from any computer, tablet or mobile device.

In general, the design will integrate the Campus Connect brand and have a simple UI to make it friendly and easy to use for students, student organizations and potential partners.
