Student Travel Guide

Useful advice to help students plan their journeys around Johannesburg.

Identify your destination before travelling. Compare transport options, check current operating times and fares, and allow extra time for delays or transfers.

Use established transport stops and ranks. Keep valuables secure, remain aware of your surroundings, and share your travel plans with someone you trust when appropriate.

Buses, trains and minibus taxis serve different areas and routes. Confirm your journey, fare, operating hours and possible transfers before travelling.
